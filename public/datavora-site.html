Common questions

Can I change plans anytime?+

Yes. Upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime. Changes take effect at the end of your current billing cycle. No penalties.

What counts as a credit?+

Each AI operation uses credits — copilot queries, insight generation, report creation. Simple uploads and chart creation are free.

Is my data secure?+

Absolutely. We use AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS in transit, and row-level security. Your data is never shared or used for training.

Do you offer a free trial of Pro?+

The free plan is your trial — no time limit. When you need more credits or features, upgrade to Pro. We also offer 14-day money-back guarantee.

Can I export my data if I leave?+

Yes. You always own your data. Export everything as CSV, JSON, or via our API at any time.

What databases do you connect to?+

PostgreSQL, MySQL, Snowflake, Databricks, and more. We're adding new connectors regularly. Enterprise plans get custom integrations.

Introduction

Welcome to DataVora. This guide will help you get up and running in under 5 minutes.

DataVora is an AI-powered analytics platform that connects to your data sources, automatically cleans and profiles your data, and provides intelligent insights through natural language queries.

Quick install

npm install datavora-sdk

Initialize the client

import { DataVora } from 'datavora-sdk';

const dv = new DataVora({
apiKey: 'your-api-key',
project: 'my-project'
});

// Upload and analyze
const dataset = await dv.upload('./data.csv');
const insights = await dv.analyze(dataset.id);
